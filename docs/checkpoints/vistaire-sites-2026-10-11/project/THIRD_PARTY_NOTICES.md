Self-hosted derived font subsets originate from the installed Linux Libertine / Linux Biolinum fonts by Philipp H. Poll, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Derived family names are Vistaire Editorial and Vistaire Text. # User-provided food and AR assets

The five added GLBs and seven supplied USDZs are user-authorized Vistaire menu assets. Original GLB binaries and embedded materials are retained. Six USDZ containers were repackaged solely to satisfy OpenUSD’s64-byte payload alignment; all41 embedded layer/texture payloads are byte-identical to uploads. The burger package is entirely unchanged. Source/served hashes are in `docs/uploaded-ar-inventory.json`. Authorization comes from the user; an independent asset license audit was not requested or performed.
